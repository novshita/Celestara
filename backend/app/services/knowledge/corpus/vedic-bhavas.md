title: Bhavas (the houses)
category: vedic
version: 1
aliases: bhava, bhavas, house, houses, whole sign houses
---
The twelve bhavas divide the chart into areas of life. In the whole-sign method the Lagna rashi is the first bhava and each following rashi is the next bhava. Traditional associations: 1 self and body; 2 resources, family and speech; 3 effort, courage and siblings; 4 home and inner security; 5 creativity, learning and children; 6 work, service and obstacles; 7 partnership; 8 change and what is hidden; 9 meaning, teachers and fortune; 10 vocation and public standing; 11 gains and community; 12 withdrawal, rest and endings.
