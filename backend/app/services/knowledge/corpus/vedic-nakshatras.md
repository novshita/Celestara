aliases: nakshatra, nakshatras, pada, lunar mansion, moon nakshatra
---
The nakshatras divide the zodiac into twenty-seven lunar mansions of thirteen degrees and twenty minutes each, and each is split into four padas of three degrees and twenty minutes. The Moon's nakshatra at birth is especially important because it sets the starting point of the Vimshottari Dasha. Each nakshatra is ruled by one of nine planets, repeating in the order Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury.
