title: Ayanamsa and the Lahiri ayanamsa
category: technical
version: 1
aliases: ayanamsa, lahiri, precession, sidereal offset, chitrapaksha
---
The ayanamsa is the angle between the tropical zodiac, tied to the March equinox, and the sidereal zodiac, tied to the stars. It exists because the equinox slowly shifts against the stars (precession) by about fifty seconds of arc a year. The Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa, the standard in India, is currently about twenty-four degrees, which is why sidereal positions are roughly one sign behind tropical ones.
