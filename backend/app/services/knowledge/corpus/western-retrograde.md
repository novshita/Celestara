title: Retrograde planets in Western astrology
category: western
version: 1
aliases: retrograde, mercury retrograde, apparent backward motion, rx
---
A planet is retrograde when it appears from Earth to move backwards through the zodiac, an effect of relative orbital speeds and not a real reversal. Mercury is retrograde about three or four times a year for roughly three weeks each time. Western tradition often reads retrograde phases as times to review, revisit and reconsider the matters that planet governs, rather than to push ahead.
