title: The Moon sign (Chandra rashi)
category: vedic
version: 1
aliases: moon sign, chandra rashi, janma rashi, rashi of the moon
---
In Vedic astrology the rashi occupied by the Moon at birth is treated as being as important as the Lagna. It is traditionally taken to describe the mind, emotional nature and habitual reactions, and it is the usual reference point for reading transits (Gochar). Because the Moon moves about thirteen degrees a day, its rashi can be uncertain when the birth time is unknown and the Moon was near the edge of a sign.
