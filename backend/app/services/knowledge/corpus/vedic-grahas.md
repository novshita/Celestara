title: The nine grahas
category: vedic
version: 1
aliases: graha, grahas, navagraha, planets, nodes
---
Vedic astrology works with nine grahas: the Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn, plus Rahu and Ketu. Rahu and Ketu are not physical bodies but the two points where the Moon's path crosses the ecliptic, always exactly 180 degrees apart. Uranus, Neptune and Pluto are not part of the traditional scheme.
