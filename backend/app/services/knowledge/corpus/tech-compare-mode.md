A comparison keeps the two systems separate and does not blend them into one. Each chart is calculated and read in its own framework: sidereal signs, whole-sign bhavas and nakshatras on the Vedic side; tropical signs, houses and aspects on the Western side. Differences between them come mainly from the ayanamsa, from the house systems, and from the different tradition of interpretation, not from an error in either.
