An aspect is a specific angle between two planets. The major aspects are the conjunction (0 degrees), sextile (60), square (90), trine (120) and opposition (180). A small allowance, the orb, lets an aspect count when the angle is close but not exact, and the orbs used are a matter of choice. Traditionally trines and sextiles are read as easier flows, squares and oppositions as tension or contrast, and conjunctions as a blending of two energies.
