---
In the whole-sign system the entire sign containing the Ascendant is the first house, the next sign the second house, and so on. Every house is exactly thirty degrees and aligns with a sign. It is the traditional Vedic method and one of the oldest Western ones, and it is simple and stable because the house of a planet changes only when it changes sign.
