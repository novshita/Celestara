The twelve houses divide the chart by area of life, starting from the Ascendant. Several systems exist. Placidus, the common default, divides time and gives houses of unequal size, and it breaks down at very high latitudes. Whole sign makes each sign a house, and equal house gives every house thirty degrees from the Ascendant. All of them depend on an accurate birth time and place, and the choice of system changes which house a planet falls in.
