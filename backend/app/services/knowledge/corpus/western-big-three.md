The Sun sign, Moon sign and Rising sign are the three most-discussed placements in a natal chart. The Sun sign is traditionally read as core identity and purpose, the Moon sign as emotional nature and needs, and the Rising sign (the Ascendant) as outward manner and first impressions. The Rising sign needs a birth time, because it changes about every two hours. A Sun sign alone describes only one factor among many.
