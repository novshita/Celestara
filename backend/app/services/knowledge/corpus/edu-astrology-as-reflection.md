Astrology is a traditional interpretive practice and is not scientifically validated as a way of predicting events. The chart positions are calculated astronomy; what they are said to mean is interpretation, and different traditions and astrologers read them differently. Guidance here is offered for reflection and exploration. It cannot say what will happen, and it should not be the basis for medical, legal, financial or other major decisions.
