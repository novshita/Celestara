title: Rahu in Vedic astrology
category: vedic
version: 1
aliases: rahu, rahu, rahu
---
Rahu, the north lunar node, is traditionally associated with ambition, appetite, novelty and the pull toward the unfamiliar. It is a calculated point, not a body, and moves backwards through the rashis in about eighteen months each.
