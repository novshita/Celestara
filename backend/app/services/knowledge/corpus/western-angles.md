The four angles are the Ascendant (rising point on the eastern horizon), the Descendant (setting point, opposite), the Midheaven or MC (the highest point of the ecliptic over the birth place) and the Imum Coeli or IC (the lowest point, opposite). They are calculated from the birth time and place, and cannot be known without them. Planets close to an angle are traditionally given extra emphasis.
