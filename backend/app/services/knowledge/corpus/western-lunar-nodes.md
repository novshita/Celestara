title: The lunar nodes in Western astrology
category: western

---
The lunar nodes are the two points where the Moon's orbit crosses the ecliptic, directly opposite each other. The North Node is traditionally read as a direction of growth and the South Node as familiar habits or past strengths. The mean node moves smoothly, while the true node oscillates around it, so the two can differ by up to about one and a half degrees.
