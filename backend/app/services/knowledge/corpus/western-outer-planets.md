category: western

Uranus, Neptune and Pluto move so slowly that everyone born within several years shares the same sign, which is why they are called generational planets. Uranus takes about seven years per sign, Neptune about fourteen, and Pluto between twelve and thirty-one because its orbit is eccentric. Their personal meaning depends mostly on the house they occupy and the aspects they make to personal planets.
