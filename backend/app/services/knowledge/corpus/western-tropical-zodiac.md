Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac, which is tied to the seasons. Zero degrees Aries is the position of the Sun at the March equinox, and the twelve signs of thirty degrees follow from there: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces. Because it follows the equinox and not the stars, it slowly drifts relative to the constellations.
