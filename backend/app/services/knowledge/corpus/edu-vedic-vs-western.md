Vedic astrology (Jyotish) uses the sidereal zodiac, the Moon's nakshatra, the Vimshottari Dasha and transits counted from the Moon, and often emphasises timing. Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac, the Sun, Moon and Rising signs, aspects and houses, and often emphasises psychology and character. Both begin from the same sky. Neither is claimed to be more correct than the other.
