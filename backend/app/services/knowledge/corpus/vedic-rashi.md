The Vedic zodiac divides the ecliptic into twelve rashis of thirty degrees each, measured against the fixed stars (the sidereal zodiac): Mesha, Vrishabha, Mithuna, Karka, Simha, Kanya, Tula, Vrischika, Dhanu, Makara, Kumbha and Meena. They correspond to Aries through Pisces by name, but because the sidereal and tropical zodiacs are offset by the ayanamsa, a planet is usually in a different sign in the two systems.
