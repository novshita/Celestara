title: Lagna (the Vedic Ascendant)
category: vedic
version: 1
aliases: ascendant, rising sign, lagna, first house
---
The Lagna is the rashi that was rising over the eastern horizon at the moment of birth. It is the starting point of the whole chart: it becomes the first bhava, and every other bhava is counted from it. It moves through a full rashi about every two hours, so it is the part of the chart most sensitive to the birth time. Traditionally it describes the person's body, temperament and approach to life.
