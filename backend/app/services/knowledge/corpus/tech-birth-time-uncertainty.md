title: What an unknown or uncertain birth time changes
category: technical
version: 1
aliases: unknown birth time, estimated birth time, uncertain time, time confidence, no birth time
---
Planet positions in signs depend only on the date and place, but the Ascendant, the houses or bhavas, the angles, the Dasha and sometimes the Moon's sign depend on the birth time. Without a birth time the Ascendant and houses cannot be calculated and are left out, not guessed. With an estimated time they are shown with that caveat, and the Moon's sign can be uncertain if it was close to a sign boundary that day.
