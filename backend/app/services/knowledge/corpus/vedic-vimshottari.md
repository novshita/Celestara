The Vimshottari Dasha divides a life into planetary periods across a 120-year cycle. The Mahadasha lengths are Ketu 7, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19 and Mercury 17 years. The first period is ruled by the lord of the Moon's nakshatra at birth, and only the part not yet elapsed remains. Each Mahadasha is subdivided into Antardashas in the same order. Traditionally the ruling planet colours the themes of its period; it describes emphasis, not fixed events.
