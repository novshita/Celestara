The sidereal zodiac (Vedic) is fixed to the stars and the tropical zodiac (Western) is fixed to the equinox. They agree on where the planets physically are; they differ only in where they start counting the signs. The offset, the ayanamsa, is about twenty-four degrees today, so roughly four in five planets fall in the previous sign in the Vedic chart compared with the Western one. Neither system is more accurate than the other: they are different frameworks with different traditions of interpretation.
