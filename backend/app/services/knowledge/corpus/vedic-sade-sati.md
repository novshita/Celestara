title: Sade Sati
category: vedic
version: 1
aliases: sade sati, sadhe sati, saturn over moon, seven and a half years
---
Sade Sati is the period of about seven and a half years during which Saturn transits the rashi before the natal Moon sign, the Moon sign itself, and the rashi after it. Tradition treats it as a time of testing, responsibility and slow consolidation. It is a widely discussed but interpretive idea; its effect is read very differently from person to person and it is not a prediction of events.
