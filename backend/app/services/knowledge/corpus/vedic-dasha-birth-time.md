title: Why the Dasha depends on the birth time
category: technical
version: 1
aliases: dasha accuracy, dasha birth time, dasha boundaries, birth time sensitivity of dasha
---
The Dasha is seeded by the Moon's exact position within its nakshatra. The Moon travels roughly thirteen degrees a day, so an error of a few minutes in the birth time moves the start and every later boundary by days or weeks, and an error of hours can change the nakshatra and so the whole sequence. Where the birth time is only estimated, period boundaries should be read as approximate.
