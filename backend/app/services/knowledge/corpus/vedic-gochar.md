Gochar is the reading of the planets' current positions against the birth chart. Traditionally the Moon sign is the main reference, counting bhavas from it, though the Lagna is used as well. Planets differ greatly in pace: the Moon changes rashi in about two and a quarter days, the Sun in a month, Mars in roughly six weeks, Jupiter in about a year, Saturn in two and a half years, and Rahu and Ketu in about eighteen months, so slow planets mark long themes and fast ones short moods.
