id: vedic-retrograde
title: Retrograde grahas (vakri)
category: vedic
version: 1
aliases: retrograde, vakri, vakra, retrograde motion
---
A graha is called vakri (retrograde) when it appears from Earth to move backwards through the zodiac. This is an effect of relative orbital motion, not a change in the planet itself. The Sun and Moon are never retrograde, and the mean Rahu and Ketu always move backwards. Traditions differ on its meaning: some regard a retrograde graha as stronger or more internalised, others as delayed or revisited themes.
